Privacy

What CabCount collects, who processes it, how long it is kept, and how to ask for deletion.

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Who we are

CabCount (cabcount.app) is an independent tracker of Tesla Robotaxi and Cybercab rollout, published by Beam42. CabCount is not affiliated with Tesla, Inc.

This page covers cabcount.app only.

Information we collect

Sighting photo and video submissions

The sightings page embeds a form hosted by Tally (tally.so/r/7XlGB6). If you submit a sighting, we collect the city, the date you saw the vehicle, what you saw (including whether a driver or safety monitor was present), any details you type, a photo or video link, and an optional email address if you want a follow-up. The details box is free text, so it can include other submitter information you choose to add. We do not publish the email address. A person reviews a submission before anything is added to the map.

Get alerts email

The “Get alerts” form posts to Kit at https://app.kit.com/forms/10069322 (the subscription endpoint is /subscriptions). We collect the email address you enter and the city you type, if you type one. Kit sends a confirmation email. Unsubscribe anytime, from a link in those emails or by asking us.

Cloudflare Web Analytics

CabCount uses Cloudflare Web Analytics. It is cookieless and does no session recording. It does not use advertising cookies. We do not use it to build a profile of you.

Hosting

Cloudflare serves the site. Like other hosts, it processes the technical data needed to deliver the page and protect the site, including IP address and the page requested. That is separate from the cookieless analytics above.

We do not sell personal information. We do not run a CabCount account system.

Processors and storage

  • Kit (the form at app.kit.com/forms/10069322) stores alert subscriptions and sends the emails.
  • Tally (tally.so) stores sighting submissions, including the photo or video link, the details you type, and the optional follow-up email, until they are deleted.
  • Cloudflare hosts cabcount.app, serves the published pages, and provides Cloudflare Web Analytics. A sighting we choose to publish becomes part of the site stored with Cloudflare. We do not keep a separate copy of submissions in our own database; this project has none.

Retention

  • Alerts. Kit keeps the subscription until you unsubscribe or we delete it after a request.
  • Sightings. Tally keeps the submission, including any photo or video link, until we delete it or you ask us to. If we publish a description or media from it, we keep that published copy until we take it down or you ask us to delete it. We do not publish the email from a sighting.
  • Analytics. We do not receive a file of identified visitors from Cloudflare Web Analytics, and we do not set our own retention period for it. There is no session recording to retain.
  • Delivery logs. Cloudflare may keep connection logs for security and operations on its own schedule. We do not copy those logs into a CabCount database.

Deletion requests

Email the privacy contact below and say what you want deleted: the email you used for alerts, the sighting you sent, or both. Include enough detail for us to find the record, such as the email address or the page URL. We will delete personal information we hold and ask Kit or Tally to delete the matching record. We may keep a short note that we received and completed the request. We may decline when the law requires us to keep the information, or when we cannot tell which record is yours.

Not for children under 13

CabCount is not for children under 13. Do not submit a sighting, an email address, or other personal information if you are under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, email the privacy contact and we will delete it.
