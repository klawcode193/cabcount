Tesla Robotaxi in Dallas, TX

City of Dallas memo dates launch April 19, 2026; Tesla says it intends to cover all of Dallas. Oct 28, 2026: the Robotaxi app is showing new riders a free first ride ("First ride on us", $0.00) in Dallas and Houston but not Austin, per an app screenshot shared on X Oct 28 (via TeslaNorth). Tesla hasn't announced it officially. Oct 30, 2026: Tesla's @robotaxi account said operating hours are now 6am to 2am, without naming cities, so check the app for this area (via Not a Tesla App). Nov 11, 2026: Tesla said Robotaxi now runs 24/7 in every city it serves (via TeslaNorth). Nov 13, 2026: Dallas is one of five Texas cities (with Austin, Fort Worth, Houston, San Antonio) where invited buyers can take delivery of a personal Cybercab (Reuters; delivery city list per Car and Driver). Nov 25, 2026: Tesla's @robotaxi account said the Dallas service area grew wider (new boundary stretches north past University Park toward Farmers Branch, Addison and Richardson), with more cars and lower fares; no new square mileage or fare given (via TeslaNorth). Nov 27, 2026: Robotaxi Tracker measured the Nov 25 expansion at about 150 sq mi, up from ~82 (Richardson-Garland Herald).
