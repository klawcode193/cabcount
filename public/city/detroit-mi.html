Tesla Robotaxi in Detroit, MI

About two dozen Cybercabs testing on Metro Detroit roads (I-696, M-10, Telegraph, Eight Mile) out of Tesla's Southfield collision center, with temporary steering wheels and human test drivers, per The Detroit News (Nov 3-4, 2026). Tesla has not confirmed it or given a public service date. First cold-weather testing market.
