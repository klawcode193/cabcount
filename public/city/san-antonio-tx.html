Tesla Robotaxi in San Antonio, TX

Tesla's @robotaxi account announced Nov 9, 2026 (just after midnight) that Robotaxi is available in San Antonio starting 5am local. Not a Tesla App reports unsupervised rides. Launch geofence about 274 sq mi, from Loop 1604 south to below downtown; airport inside the zone but not served. Tesla hasn't said fleet size, pricing, or whether Cybercab runs here; Robotaxi Tracker had logged no San Antonio cars as of Monday morning.
