Tesla Robotaxi in Fort Worth, TX

Tesla's @robotaxi account posted Nov 1, 2026: "Robotaxi available in Fort Worth starting tomorrow, 6am," with a launch geofence map centered on downtown (north toward Saginaw, west past White Settlement, southwest to Benbrook, east edge near Haltom City and Forest Hill). Not a Tesla App reports rides are unsupervised with no safety monitor. Not on Tesla's Oct 21 coming-soon list. The zone is not connected to Dallas. Tesla hasn't said how many cars serve Fort Worth, pricing, or whether Cybercab runs here; vehicle type listed as Model Y until confirmed.
