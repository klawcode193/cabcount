About CabCount

CabCount is an independent, source-linked tracker of Tesla's Robotaxi and Cybercab rollout.

Not affiliated with Tesla

CabCount is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Tesla, Inc. "Tesla", "Robotaxi" and "Cybercab" are trademarks of their owners.

Methodology

  • Fleet numbers are registrations. Counts come from Texas DMV registration records as summarized by public trackers and news — they are not cars confirmed driving riders.
  • Service areas are estimates. Tesla doesn't publish geofence sizes; figures are traced from the in-app map and differ by a few percent between trackers.
  • Statuses:
    Live – no monitor — Public rides, at least some cars with no safety monitor
    Live – safety driver — Public rides, human safety driver in every car
    Preparing / Permitted — Tesla-confirmed 'Preparations Underway' or permitted, no public riders yet
    Testing / Sightings — Vehicles spotted / staged, no Tesla confirmation of a launch
    Requested — No Tesla activity known; readers asking for it (demand signal only)
  • Requested cities are reader demand only — they're placed approximately and imply no Tesla activity.

Other metrics

  • Cumulative unsupervised miles: 1,000,000+ (as of Sep 3, 2026)
  • NHTSA Cybercab review: Audit Query AQ26002 (2026-09-03) escalated to Special Order (2026-09-10); sworn answers due 2026-09-30; no outcome announced as of 2026-10-04
