Subject: CabCount #3: Fort Worth is Tesla's 7th driverless city
Preview text: Tesla's Robotaxi tracker, week of Nov 2

**The count this week**
- 8 cities with public rides: 7 driverless (Austin, Dallas, Fort Worth, Houston, Miami, Orlando, Tampa) plus the Bay Area with a safety driver. 4 more listed by Tesla as coming soon (Phoenix, Las Vegas, Jacksonville, San Antonio).
- 1,244 Robotaxis registered in Texas as of Nov 1: 671 Model Y and 573 Cybercab. That's up 274 from 970 a week ago. The week started with 110 more Cybercabs (passing 1,000 for the first time on Oct 27), then Tesla added 164 Model Ys, its first new Model Y registrations since mid-October. These are registrations, not cars confirmed on the road. [fsddb.com](https://fsddb.com/robotaxi?operator=tesla-robotaxi-llc&service=all)
- Cybercab rides are still public only in Austin. Tesla says Dallas gets them this month.
See every city on the map: [cabcount.app](https://cabcount.app)

**City of the week: Fort Worth**
Tesla's first new city since July went live this morning at 6am. "Robotaxi available in Fort Worth starting tomorrow, 6am," the official Robotaxi account posted Sunday night. Rides are unsupervised, with no safety monitor in the car. The launch zone is centered on downtown and reaches Saginaw to the north, White Settlement to the west, Benbrook to the southwest, and Haltom City and Forest Hill to the east. Tesla didn't give a size: Sawyer Merritt puts it at about 143 square miles, which would make it Tesla's third-biggest driverless zone, while the Robotaxi Log tracing says about 104. It doesn't connect to the Dallas zone, so you can't ride between the two. Fort Worth wasn't even on Tesla's Oct 21 coming-soon list. Tesla hasn't said how many cars serve it or what rides cost. **Later nights**
On Oct 30 the Robotaxi account said operating hours are now 6am to 2am, up from an 11pm cutoff earlier in October. It didn't name cities, so check the app where you are. [Not a Tesla App](https://www.notateslaapp.com/news/4920/tesla-extends-robotaxi-hours-to-2-am-adds-new-calm-mode-that-hides-fsd-visuals)

**Houston, mapped**
Last week's bigger Houston zone now measures about 254 square miles, roughly 10 times the launch zone, per the Robotaxi Tracker. One catch: Electrek reports the app still won't pick up in parts of downtown inside the new map. [Houston on CabCount](https://cabcount.app/city/houston-tx)

**A free first ride?**
New riders in Dallas and Houston have been seeing "First ride on us" ($0) in the app, per a screenshot shared on X (via TeslaNorth). Tesla hasn't announced it, and it isn't showing in Austin. [TeslaNorth](https://teslanorth.com/2026/10/29/robotaxi-free-first-ride/)

**Coming up**
- Cybercab rides in Dallas, which Tesla said on its Oct 21 earnings call would start this month.
- Tesla's sworn answers to NHTSA's review of how it certified the Cybercab were due Oct 30. They aren't public yet, and the review is still open.
