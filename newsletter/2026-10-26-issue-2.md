Subject: CabCount #2: Houston's Robotaxi zone just got a lot bigger
Preview text: Tesla's Robotaxi tracker, week of Oct 26

**The count this week**
- 7 cities with public rides: 6 driverless (Austin, Dallas, Houston, Miami, Orlando, Tampa) plus the Bay Area with a safety driver. 4 more listed by Tesla as coming soon (Phoenix, Las Vegas, Jacksonville, San Antonio).
- 970 Robotaxis registered in Texas as of Oct 24: 507 Model Y and 463 Cybercab. That's up 112 from 858 on Oct 21, and every one of the new registrations is a Cybercab. These are registrations, not cars confirmed on the road. [fsddb.com](https://fsddb.com/robotaxi?operator=tesla-robotaxi-llc&service=all)
- Cybercab rides are public in 1 city, Austin. Dallas is next, which Tesla says is coming in November.
See every city on the map: [cabcount.app](https://cabcount.app)

**City of the week: Houston**
This morning Tesla switched on a much bigger Houston service area ("Houston, we have liftoff," from the official Robotaxi account). The old zone was a roughly 25-square-mile patch in northwest Houston around Jersey Village and Willowbrook. The new one keeps that and stretches southeast through central Houston and downtown toward Bellaire. Tesla didn't give a new square-mile figure or say whether hours changed. Yes, the map is shaped like a Starship, and Elon says that's "just a coincidence." Houston still has no public Cybercab rides, even though about 200 Cybercabs were filmed staged at a Houston-area lot earlier this month. **Austin's F1 weekend**
Ahead of the US Grand Prix, Tesla stretched the Austin zone to reach Circuit of the Americas and moved last call to 1am. Tesla hasn't said whether either change sticks after race weekend. The Cybercab Spotter account logged a record 1,250 Cybercab sightings in Austin on qualifying day (Oct 24), up from the old high of 1,072 on Oct 16. Sightings count the same car more than once, so that measures how busy the fleet was, not how big it is. [Teslarati](https://www.teslarati.com/tesla-cybercabs-take-over-austins-f1-weekend/) · [TeslaNorth](https://teslanorth.com/2026/10/23/tesla-robotaxi-austin-1am-cota/)

**San Antonio is getting ready**
Tesla's first-responder lookup now lists 19 Model Y Robotaxis in San Antonio. It's still "coming soon," with no public rides yet.

**Coming up**
- Tesla's answers to NHTSA's review of how it self-certified the Cybercab (no steering wheel or pedals) are due Oct 30.
- Cybercab rides in Dallas are expected next month, and Tesla says Austin hours go to 2am "pretty soon."
