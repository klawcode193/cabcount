Subject: CabCount #1: 858 Robotaxis registered in Texas, Cybercab heads to Dallas
Preview text: Tesla's Robotaxi tracker, week of Oct 22

**The count this week**
- 7 cities with public rides: 6 driverless (Austin, Dallas, Houston, Miami, Orlando, Tampa) plus the Bay Area with a safety driver. 2 more preparing (Phoenix, Las Vegas).
- 858 Robotaxis registered in Texas as of Oct 21: 507 Model Y and 351 Cybercab. That's up 89 from 769 on Oct 16 (+87 Model Y, +2 Cybercab). These are registrations, not cars confirmed on the road. [fsddb.com](https://fsddb.com/robotaxi?operator=tesla-robotaxi-llc&service=all)
- Cybercab rides are public in 1 city, Austin.
See every city on the map: [cabcount.app](https://cabcount.app)

**Cybercab is coming to Dallas next month**
On Tesla's Oct 21 earnings call, Ashok Elluswamy said: "next month, we'll be launching in Dallas and then the same software just scaled to lots of other cities along with the Model Y Robotaxis." Vaibhav Taneja added that Tesla plans to bring Cybercab to "some other cities before the end of this year." [Call transcript](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/10/22/tesla-tsla-q3-2026-earnings-call-transcript/)

**4 cities now listed as "coming soon"**
Tesla's new Robotaxi safety page names Las Vegas, Phoenix, Jacksonville and San Antonio as coming soon, with no dates. Las Vegas already has a Nevada permit for up to 5,000 cars. [TeslAnt](https://teslant.com/en/news/tesla-robotaxi-safety-report-3-1-million-miles-32-crashes-las-vegas-phoenix-next-2026-10)

**Tesla's first Robotaxi safety report**
Published Oct 21: 3.1 million miles with no safety monitor aboard from Jan 22 to Oct 18, about two-thirds of them in Austin. Tesla lists 32 reported collisions, 30 with property damage only, and says no one was injured. [TeslAnt](https://teslant.com/en/news/tesla-robotaxi-safety-report-3-1-million-miles-32-crashes-las-vegas-phoenix-next-2026-10)

**Incidents and regulators**
- The first Cybercab crash report is in NHTSA's data: an August test drive at about 5 mph into a water-filled pothole in an Austin parking lot. Safety monitor aboard, no passengers, property damage only. It happened before public rides started. [Teslanewswire](https://teslanewswire.com/articles/tesla-robotaxi-nhtsa-crash-data-august-2026-first-cybercab-crash-dallas-austin)
- NHTSA's review of how Tesla self-certified the Cybercab (no steering wheel or pedals) is still open. Tesla's answers are due Oct 30. [Reuters via Investing.com](https://za.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/tesla-aims-to-sell-cybercab-robotaxis-by-end-of-year-despite-federal-probe-4518097)

**Rider changes**
- Austin hours are now 6am to midnight. On the call, Tesla said hours will go to 2am "pretty soon," with 24/7 service planned for later this year. [Call transcript](https://evwire.com/p/tesla-tsla-q3-2026-earnings-call-full-transcript)

**Cybercab production**
- Lars Moravy said Tesla is "trying to get Cybercabs to customers by the end of this year," but for now every Cybercab built in Texas goes to Tesla's own Robotaxi fleet. Elon Musk added that most Cybercabs will stay Tesla-owned. [Call transcript](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/10/22/tesla-tsla-q3-2026-earnings-call-transcript/)
- Elon said the Austin Cybercab fleet "has grown by a factor of 10" since launch. [Call transcript](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/10/22/tesla-tsla-q3-2026-earnings-call-transcript/)
